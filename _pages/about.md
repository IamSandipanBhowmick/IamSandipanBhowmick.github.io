I am **Sandipan Bhowmick**, a PhD scholar in Computer Science and Engineering. My research lies at the intersection of **spiking neural networks**, **neuromorphic computing**, **medical image classification**, **computability theory**, and **computational complexity theory**.

My current work focuses on lightweight brain-inspired spiking neural networks for energy-aware medical image classification. I am also interested in theoretical foundations of neuromorphic computation, including abstract models of neuromorphic algorithms, energy-aware computation, decidability, and complexity-theoretic analysis.

## Research Interests

- Spiking Neural Networks
- Neuromorphic Computing
- Energy-aware Deep Learning
- Medical Image Classification
- Computability Theory
- Computational Complexity Theory
- Neuromorphic Turing Machine Models

## Current Research Direction

I am interested in developing both practical and theoretical foundations for neuromorphic computation. On the applied side, I work on lightweight spiking neural networks for medical image classification. On the theoretical side, I study computability and complexity-theoretic questions related to neuromorphic systems.
